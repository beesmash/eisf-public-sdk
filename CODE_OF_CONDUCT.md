# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to collaborate professionally and in good faith.

Be respectful of differing experience levels and viewpoints. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of private or confidential information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the integrity of the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers through an appropriate contact channel published by Beesmash Inc.
